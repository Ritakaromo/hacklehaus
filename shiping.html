Shipment

At HackleHausFly Co., we’re committed to delivering your orders swiftly, securely, and transparently. We partner with trusted carriers to ensure reliable service for all our customers.

  • Carriers: DHL and local delivery partners.
  • Tracking: Receive a tracking link via email once your order is processed.
  • Delivery Times:
    • Kenya: 2–5 business days
    • International: 7–14 business days
  • Shipping Fees: Vary by location and order size. Free delivery available for qualifying purchases.
  • Packaging: Eco-friendly, waterproof materials. Items may ship together or separately depending on availability.
  • International Orders: Customs duties and taxes may apply, paid by recipient.
  • Delays: HackleHausFly Co. is not liable for external delays (e.g., weather, customs clearance).
