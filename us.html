About Us

Nestled at the crossroads of the Equator and the wild African waters, HackleHausFly Co was born with a simple mission: to craft artisan fly-fishing gear inspired by Kenya’s untamed beauty and passed-down traditions.

Our journey began along the banks of the Tana River, where stories of legendary trout catches were whispered between generations of anglers. Today, we honor those tales with every fly we tie—precision-crafted using locally sourced feathers, thread, and spirit.

At HackleHaus, we blend time-honored African fly-tying methods with innovative design to deliver world-class quality that’s as ready for the Aberdares as it is for the Alps. Whether you're knee-deep in Lake Naivasha or standing in the brisk waters of British Columbia, our flies are trusted to deliver.

This isn’t just fishing gear—it’s a living connection to land, water, and tradition. Made in Kenya. Cast anywhere.

Background

Founded on the fertile banks of the Sagana River, HackleHausFly Co emerged from a love for nature, a passion for fishing, and a deep respect for Kenyan heritage. What began as a riverside hobby between a father and son has grown into a full-fledged artisan fly-tying workshop serving anglers across the globe.

Our roots are as wild and resilient as the African waters we fish. We started small—crafting a few handmade flies for friends and local lodges. But as word spread, so did our vision. Guided by East African rivers and inspired by mountain streams, we developed a uniquely Kenyan style: bold, precise, and forged for the thrill of the catch.

Today, HackleHausFly Co combines traditional African fly-tying methods with modern design, sustainable sourcing, and global shipping. From Lake Turkana to Patagonia, our work has traveled farther than we ever dreamed—and it's only the beginning.

Why choose Us

HackleHausFly Co is proudly rooted in Kenya—where our rivers, lakes, and coastlines inspire every fly we tie. Our flies are hand-crafted using locally sourced materials and generations of fly-tying knowledge from East Africa. Whether you're casting in Samburu, drifting the Tana River, or standing knee-deep in Lake Naivasha, our gear is made to perform.

We combine local tradition with world-class standards—ensuring each fly is built for durability, precision, and the thrill of the catch. And the best part? We ship worldwide. From Nairobi to the US, Canada, Norway, Asia our flies are trusted by anglers around the globe.
