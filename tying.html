Custom Fly Tying Orders

At HackleHausFly Co, we know that every angler has unique preferences. That’s why we offer custom fly tying services tailored to your fishing style, target species, and waters. Whether it’s a subtle variation of a classic dry fly or a fully custom design, we’ll tie flies to match your exact needs.

What We Offer

  • Any Fly Pattern: Classic dries, nymphs, emergers, terrestrials, and streamers.
  • Color & Size Options: Choose your hook size, body color, wing variation, or material preference.
  • Custom Requests: We can adapt patterns for tougher waters, larger fish, or selective trout.
  • Pricing: Custom flies start at KSh 700 / $5.00 each (price may vary by complexity).
  • Turnaround: Small custom sets usually ship in 3–7 days. Bulk orders may take longer.

How to Place a Custom Order

Simply send us an email with your order details, and we’ll reply with a quote and delivery timeline.

Please include:

  • Fly Pattern (e.g., Stimulator, Custom Hopper, etc.)
  • Preferred Colors
  • Hook Size
  • Quantity
  • Any special notes (materials, durability, waters, target species, etc.)

📧 Email your custom order to: orders@hacklehausfly.co

We’ll tie your flies with the same attention to detail and craftsmanship that goes into every pattern in our shop. Your custom flies are guaranteed to be fish-ready and durable.
